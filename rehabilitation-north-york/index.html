Health · North York

Rehabilitation

Every discipline, one coordinated plan.

Recovery rarely fits inside one discipline. iBODY brings physiotherapy, chiropractic care, registered massage and acupuncture under one roof — so your plan draws on whichever combination actually serves your recovery.

How coordinated care works

One assessment sets the plan. Your practitioners share notes and adjust together — the physiotherapist knows what the RMT found, the chiropractor knows what improved last week. You don’t repeat your story at every door.

Who we help

Accident and workplace injuries, post-surgical recovery, chronic pain, sports injuries, and mobility challenges from arthritis or aging — see the conditions we treat.

Common questions

Answers before you book

What does a multidisciplinary rehab clinic mean?

It means physiotherapy, chiropractic, massage and acupuncture work under one roof on one shared plan — your practitioners read each other's notes and adjust together, instead of you carrying your story between separate clinics.

Which rehab discipline should I start with?

Book an assessment with any of our practitioners and describe your situation — if a different discipline should lead, we say so and hand you over properly. You can also read our guide on choosing a practitioner.

Do you treat chronic pain or only injuries?

Both. Chronic pain often responds best to exactly this kind of coordinated care — movement-based treatment, hands-on work and pain-modulation approaches combined, with a plan that adjusts as you progress.
