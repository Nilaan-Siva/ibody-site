WSIB Physiotherapy

Workplace injury care, billed directly to WSIB.

If you were injured at work in Ontario, WSIB covers your physiotherapy — and we bill WSIB directly, so treatment costs you nothing. Bring your claim number to your first visit and we take it from there.

How WSIB treatment works

We assess your injury, build a treatment plan aligned with WSIB’s programs of care, and submit the required reporting. Treatment focuses on safe, progressive return to your job — including modified-duties guidance where appropriate.

No claim number yet?

Report your injury to your employer and file with WSIB as soon as possible — treatment can usually begin while your claim is processed. Call us at 647-710-6927 and we’ll walk you through it.

Common questions

Answers before you book

Does WSIB pay for my physiotherapy?

Yes — for approved workplace injury claims, WSIB covers treatment and we bill WSIB directly, so it costs you nothing. Bring your claim number to your first visit.

Can I start treatment before my WSIB claim is approved?

Usually, yes. Report the injury to your employer and file with WSIB right away — treatment can typically begin while your claim is processed. Call us and we'll walk you through it.

Do I get to choose my own physiotherapy clinic for WSIB?

Yes. In Ontario you have the right to choose your health care provider for a workplace injury. iBODY treats WSIB patients regularly and handles all required reporting.
