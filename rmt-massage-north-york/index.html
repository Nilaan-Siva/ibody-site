Registered Massage Therapy

Therapeutic treatment — not just relaxation.

Our Registered Massage Therapists treat muscle tension, chronic pain, injury recovery and stress — with techniques chosen for your condition, from deep tissue and trigger-point work to lymphatic drainage.

Covered by your benefits

RMT massage is one of the most commonly covered services in extended health plans. We direct bill most insurers, so your visit is often no-cost out of pocket. Bring your policy details to your first visit and we confirm coverage before treatment.

Part of your recovery plan

Massage therapy pairs naturally with physiotherapy and chiropractic care — releasing the tissue restrictions that hold back progress. It is also part of our body sculpting program, supporting lymphatic drainage and circulation.

Common questions

Answers before you book

Is massage therapy covered by my benefits?

RMT massage is one of the most commonly covered services in extended health plans, and we direct bill most insurers — many patients pay nothing at their visit. Bring your policy details and we confirm coverage before treatment.

What's the difference between an RMT and a spa massage?

A Registered Massage Therapist is a regulated health professional whose treatment targets your specific condition — injury recovery, chronic tension, circulation. It's therapeutic care that qualifies for insurance coverage, not just relaxation.

How long is a massage therapy session?

We offer 30, 45 and 60-minute treatments. First visits include a short health history so your RMT can target the treatment safely — arrive a few minutes early.
