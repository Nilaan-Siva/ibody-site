Physiotherapy

Assessment-first care that gets you moving again.

Our physiotherapy programs help patients recover from injury, manage chronic conditions, and improve their overall quality of life — from motor vehicle and workplace injuries to everyday pain that has overstayed its welcome.

What to expect

Your first visit runs 45 to 60 minutes: a full history, a hands-on movement and strength assessment, a plain-language explanation of what we find, and treatment the same day. You leave with a written plan — how many visits, what each one is for, and what to do between them.

What we treat

Back and neck pain, joint sprains and muscle strains, post-surgical recovery, sports injuries, arthritis, sciatica, whiplash and more. If your condition needs a different practitioner, we say so and refer you within the clinic — see which practitioner is right for me.

Coverage

No doctor’s referral is needed in Ontario. We direct bill most extended health plans, WSIB, and auto insurers for accident claims. Book online or call 647-710-6927 — free parking at 726 Gordon Baker Road, and we direct bill most extended health plans.

Common questions

Answers before you book

Do I need a referral to see a physiotherapist in North York?

No. Physiotherapists are primary care practitioners in Ontario, so you can book directly with iBODY. Some extended health plans ask for a doctor's note before reimbursing — check your policy or ask us to help confirm.

How much does physiotherapy cost with insurance?

Most patients pay nothing out of pocket at their visit — we direct bill Sun Life, Canada Life, Green Shield, Blue Cross and most major plans, plus WSIB and auto insurers for accident claims.

How many physiotherapy sessions will I need?

It depends on your condition — after your first 45–60 minute assessment you leave with a written plan stating how many visits we recommend and what each one is for. Simple strains often need 4–6 visits; post-surgical recovery takes longer.

Is physiotherapy covered after a car accident in Ontario?

Yes. Ontario's statutory accident benefits cover physiotherapy through your own auto insurer regardless of fault, and we complete the OCF paperwork for you.
