Our approach

Non-invasive, by registered practitioners

Every treatment in this program is non-surgical and needle-free, delivered by registered practitioners in a clinical setting.

Your first visit starts with a consultation, not a sales pitch: we map your goals, build a written plan, and combine only the treatments your body actually needs.

Common questions

Answers before you book

Does it hurt, and is there downtime?

No. Every treatment in the program is non-invasive — most people describe EMS as an intense workout sensation and i-Lipo as warmth. You can return to your day immediately.

Is any of this covered by insurance?

Aesthetic contouring generally isn’t, but massage therapy with our RMTs may be covered by your extended health plan — we direct bill most insurers.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your goals — your first visit includes an assessment and a written plan.
