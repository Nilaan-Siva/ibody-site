Car Accident (MVA) Rehabilitation

Focus on recovering. We handle the paperwork.

After a collision in Ontario, your own auto insurer covers your rehabilitation through statutory accident benefits — regardless of who was at fault. iBODY has treated MVA patients for years: we know the system, the forms and the timelines.

What’s covered

Physiotherapy, chiropractic care and massage therapy for accident injuries — commonly whiplash, back and neck pain, headaches, and soft-tissue injuries. Coverage applies to drivers, passengers, cyclists and pedestrians.

We complete the OCF forms for you

The accident-benefits system runs on OCF forms — treatment plans, invoices, progress reports. We complete and submit them directly to your insurer, so your energy goes into recovery, not paperwork.

Start soon

Early treatment matters — both for your recovery and your claim. Report the accident to your insurer, then call us at 647-710-6927; we can usually see you within the week.

Common questions

Answers before you book

Who pays for physiotherapy after a car accident in Ontario?

Your own auto insurer, through statutory accident benefits — regardless of who was at fault. This applies to drivers, passengers, cyclists and pedestrians. Your work benefits stay untouched.

What are OCF forms and do I have to fill them out?

OCF forms are the paperwork of Ontario's accident-benefits system — treatment plans, invoices, progress reports. We complete and submit them directly to your insurer for you.

How soon after a car accident should I start treatment?

Within days if possible. Early treatment leads to better recovery — and delays can complicate both your symptoms and your claim. Report the accident to your insurer, then call us; we can usually see you within the week.
