Body Contouring

Planned around your goals — with a realistic estimate up front.

Body contouring targets the shape of specific areas — waist, hips, arms, thighs — rather than the number on the scale. It’s the right tool when your weight is where you want it, but the silhouette isn’t.

How it works at iBODY

Your first visit is an assessment: we map the areas you want to address and give you a realistic estimate of how many sessions it takes. Sessions are non-invasive and comfortable, and most patients combine contouring with muscle stimulation or low-level laser (i-Lipo) for compounding results — see the full Body Sculpting Program.

Is it for you?

Contouring works best for targeted shaping, not overall weight loss. If you’re unsure which treatment fits your goals, book a consultation and we’ll tell you honestly — including if the answer is “you don’t need this.”

Common questions

Answers before you book

Is body contouring the same as weight loss?

No — contouring shapes specific areas (waist, hips, arms, thighs) rather than lowering the number on the scale. It's the right tool when your weight is where you want it but the silhouette isn't.

How many body contouring sessions will I need?

It depends on the area and your goals — at your first assessment we give you a realistic estimate rather than selling a package. Most areas need a series of sessions to show lasting change.

Does body contouring hurt or need downtime?

No — sessions are non-invasive and comfortable, and you can return to your day immediately.
