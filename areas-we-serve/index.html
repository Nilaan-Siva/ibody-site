iBODY · North York

Areas We Serve

One clinic, minutes from four communities.

iBODY sits at 726 Gordon Baker Road — right where North York meets Scarborough and Markham, just south of Steeles near Victoria Park Avenue. That corner location means we’re a short drive from four communities, with free parking at the door when you arrive.

North York

Our home neighbourhood. Patients come to us from Hillcrest Village, Don Valley Village, Bayview Woods and along the Finch and Steeles corridors — most within a 10-minute drive. Every service we offer, from physiotherapy to the medical spa, is delivered here.

Scarborough

We’re steps from the Scarborough border — for patients around L’Amoreaux, Agincourt and Milliken, we’re often closer than clinics labelled “Scarborough.” MVA and WSIB patients especially appreciate the quick access from Highway 404 and Victoria Park.

Markham & Thornhill

Just across Steeles, Markham and Thornhill patients reach us in minutes via Victoria Park, Woodbine or the 404. Direct billing works exactly the same regardless of which side of Steeles you live on.

Richmond Hill

A straight run down the 404 brings Richmond Hill patients to our door — many combine appointments with errands in the area. Same-week appointments are usually available.

Getting here

Free on-site parking, wheelchair-accessible entrance, and transit access via Steeles and Victoria Park routes. See hours, map and contact details.

Common questions

Answers before you book

Do you serve patients outside North York?

Yes — our clinic sits where North York, Scarborough and Markham meet, so patients come from all three plus Thornhill and Richmond Hill. Direct billing and every service work the same wherever you live.

Is there parking at the clinic?

Yes, free on-site parking at the door at 726 Gordon Baker Road — no meters, no garages.

How do I get there from Highway 404?

Exit at Steeles Avenue East, head east to Victoria Park Avenue, then south to Gordon Baker Road — about three minutes from the highway.
