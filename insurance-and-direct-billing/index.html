Insurance & Direct Billing

Most patients pay nothing out of pocket at their visit.

We bill your insurer directly whenever your plan allows it — you bring your policy and member number to your first visit, we confirm your coverage before treatment begins, and the paperwork happens behind the scenes.

WSIB claims

Injured at work? We treat approved WSIB claims and bill the Workplace Safety and Insurance Board directly — just bring your claim number to your first visit.

Car accident (MVA) claims

In Ontario, treatment after a motor vehicle accident is covered through your own auto insurer under statutory accident benefits — regardless of who was at fault. We complete the OCF-1, OCF-18 and OCF-23 forms on your behalf and deal with the insurer directly — read more about MVA rehab.

If your plan isn’t listed

Call us at 647-710-6927 with your provider’s name and we will confirm your coverage before you book — direct-billing networks change often, and where direct billing isn’t possible we provide detailed receipts for easy reimbursement.
