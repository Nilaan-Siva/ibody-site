Lash Extensions

Classic, hybrid and volume — applied lash by lash.

Classic sets for natural definition, hybrid and volume sets for fullness — applied lash by lash with proper isolation, quality adhesives and the patience the work deserves. Your natural lashes stay healthy; that’s the non-negotiable.

What to expect

A full set takes about two hours of comfortable, eyes-closed rest. Fills every two to three weeks keep the set full as your natural lashes cycle. We’ll match curl, length and density to your eye shape and daily style.

Lash health first

Overloaded extensions damage natural lashes. If your lashes need a lighter set — or a break — we’ll say so. Long-term clients come from honest advice, not maximum volume.

Common questions

Answers before you book

How long do lash extensions last?

A full set lasts through your natural lash cycle, with fills every 2–3 weeks keeping it full. With proper aftercare, sets stay clean and comfortable between fills.

Will lash extensions damage my natural lashes?

Not when applied properly — one extension per isolated natural lash, weighted to what your lashes can carry. If your lashes need a lighter set or a break, we say so; lash health is the non-negotiable.

Classic, hybrid or volume — what's the difference?

Classic adds one extension per lash for natural definition; volume uses light multi-lash fans for fullness; hybrid mixes both. We match the style to your eye shape and daily look at your appointment.
